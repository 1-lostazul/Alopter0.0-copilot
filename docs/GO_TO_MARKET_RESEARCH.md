# Go-to-Market Research — AIopter

## 1) App idea and target audience
- **App idea:** AIopter is a floating, permission-first Android assistant that helps users understand and act on what they see in other apps.
- **Primary audience:** Android users who multitask often (students, professionals, creators, and support/operations users).
- **Secondary audience:** Users who need quick summaries, translation, and drafting help without switching apps.

## 2) User problems to validate
- Too much context switching between apps while researching or composing responses.
- Difficulty extracting useful information quickly from long screens or messages.
- Trust concerns with AI assistants that do not clearly expose permissions and status.

## 3) Google Play keyword hypotheses
- floating ai assistant
- ai screen helper
- android ai overlay
- ask ai on screen
- summarize screen text
- ai translation android
- ai reply helper

## 4) Competitor scan starter
- Review direct/adjacent apps in Play categories containing: assistant, overlay, AI chat, OCR helper.
- Compare:
  - Value proposition clarity in first screenshot and first two lines of description.
  - Permission explanation quality.
  - Rating volume and recurring complaints.
  - Monetization style (ads, subscription, one-time unlock).

## 5) Validation loop
1. Publish a closed-testing MVP.
2. Track install conversion, onboarding completion, D1 retention, and crash-free users.
3. Collect top 10 user feedback themes from Play reviews and in-app feedback.
4. Update keywords, screenshots, and store description monthly.
